NeoHub

neohub.base44.app

NeoHub is a sleek command center that unifies an entire productivity suite behind a single door. Five precision tools, one interface, zero context switching — launch anything with ⌘K, track uptime and endpoints at a glance, and keep every workflow humming from documents to spreadsheets. It's the front desk for the whole Neo toolkit, built to feel fast, calm and distraction-free from the very first click.

📄NeoDocsReal-time collaborative documents with rich formatting, comments and sharing baked in. 🎞️NeoSlidesA clean, distraction-free slide editor for building presentations that actually look designed. 📋NeoFormsSmart forms and surveys with conditional logic, validation and instant analytics on responses. 📊NeoSheetsA high-performance spreadsheet with formulas, charts and real-time collaboration.
